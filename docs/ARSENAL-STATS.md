# Arsenal stats: kills per item

Generated by `node tools/arsenal-stats.mjs` on 2026-10-23 (version 0.16.0+8ae4996). Raw runs: `ARSENAL-STATS.json`. Measurement only: no balance was changed to get these numbers.

**Setup.** Bot pilots (Pro, All-Star, Champion) play full runs from a fresh profile with the whole arsenal on offer, 5 runs per level and difficulty (30 runs), stopping at game over or wave 40. The per-wave Zombies weather is on (the laser needs clear air). They are the benchmark's teleporting pilots (`tools/zbot.mjs`): a pilot's burst is a dice roll with the level's skill, so read the weapon rows as "what the weapon does in a competent bot's hands", not as a human's ranking.

**Columns.** *Bought*: purchases over all runs. *Kills*: paid kills booked to the item that made them (a pilot weapon: its bursts, the dart's contact, the bomblets; a defence: its own shots, drones, pulses, snags, beams), *Share* of all kills in that mode. *Kills/purchase* = Kills / Bought. *Kills/$1000*: kills per $1,000 spent on it (purchase plus upgrades and refits; upkeep from wave 11 is not counted), *Pay/$*: what those kills paid into the purse per $1 spent (a helper's kill pays 70% of a pilot's; a swarm drone pays $20, a Shahed $100). *Waves*: waves the weapon was loaded / defence-waves it stood. *Kills/wave* = Kills / Waves. *Pick rate*: share of the runs that reached the item's unlock wave that bought it at least once. *Targets*: the enemy kinds its kills were made on (3 biggest, 5% and up).

## Reading

Written by hand for the run above (30 bot runs, waves 13-40). A rerun moves the numbers; the shape should hold. The tool keeps this section when it rewrites the tables.

**Read the kills with care.**
- 57% of all kills are FPV swarm drones, which pay $20 each, so anything that kills swarms scores high on kills and low on Pay/$. Cash per kill differs by 5x between a swarm drone and a Shahed, and 17x to a cruise missile.
- The pilot's weapons together make about 5% of the kills in both modes (the standard warhead alone 1%). The Airstrike perk makes 11-13%, more than every pilot weapon combined. These pilots are the benchmark's teleporting bots, so this is "a competent pilot", not a ranking for a human.
- A weapon's kills are not extra kills: the standard warhead would have taken most of the same tracks. Its Kills/$1000 and Pay/$ look huge (the pod: 88 and 36) because the price is paid once and every kill while it is loaded is booked to it. Kills per wave are also skewed by when a bot loads a weapon (Champion loads cluster, pod and EMP late, in the big waves).
- Support is invisible: smoke and the jammer kill nothing by design, the EMP's real job is the 60% bonus its helpers get on disabled tracks (only 17 such kills in 30 runs), and the pod's chaff decoyed 43 tracks in all.
- The bots decide what gets bought. Pro only ever picks flechette or net, All-Star values weapons with noise, Champion weighs all of them, so a pick rate says how this policy rates an item. Run-to-run spread is wide (Pro Normal ended at waves 15 to 33).

**Pull their weight.**
- Gepard (stock, 26-35% of all kills, 190 kills per purchase) and the drone rack (17-20%, but only 8-9 kills/$1000 because of the upgrades) carry the game; the arsenal comes second.
- Laser battery is the best arsenal item: bought in 87% (Normal) and 67% (Hard) of eligible runs, 4.8 to 8.4 kills a wave, 126 to 199 kills per purchase, and the only arsenal defence that pays its price back in Hard (Pay/$ 1.2). It is mostly a swarm killer (swarm 75-80% of its kills).
- Drone carrier: bought in 93% of runs, 80 to 124 kills per purchase, 4 to 5 kills a wave, the widest target mix of the arsenal (swarm 45%, Shahed 27-31%, jet 13%).
- Flechette is the working pilot weapon: 25-29 kills per purchase and 28-32 kills/$1000, spread over jets, cruise missiles and swarms. The cluster warhead is the best per loaded wave (5 to 8) but only Champion buys it and 53-61% of its kills are swarm drones.

**Marginal.**
- Microwave dome: swarm only (90-97% of its kills), 8.6 kills/$1000 on Normal and a Pay/$ of 0.13 to 0.33. It does better on Hard (25 kills/$1000) where swarms come earlier. The Airstrike perk already clears the same targets.
- Balloon barrage: cheap and picked in every run (it is in every bot's shopping list) but 5 to 12 kills per purchase and 0.4 to 1.1 kills a wave. The stock flak mortar is close to it on Normal (6 kills per purchase) and better on Hard (18).
- Net drone: 33% pick (Pro only), 40 kills in 10 purchases, 67-79% of them Shaheds; it only matters in waves 2 to 9.
- Kinetic dart: 12 purchases, 101 kills, 8 to 9 per purchase. Its value is the target mix, not the count: cruise missiles 56% on Normal, then ballistic missiles and boss units.
- The domes (stock) make few kills (15 to 20 per purchase) but those are missiles and glide bodies that nothing else stops, so kills undersell them.

**Never or almost never picked.**
- EW jammer (stock): 0 of 30 runs. Smoke generators: 2 of 30 (both Hard, Champion and All-Star), 0 kills by design and 0 dives diverted in 52 defence-waves. In a probe run (Champion, Normal, waves 5 to 14, three screens on the substation positions) tracks were in a dive within 600 m of a screen for about 15 seconds in total and none was diverted, which suggests the helpers usually kill tracks before they dive.
- EMP warhead: 6 purchases, 13 kills, two thirds of them boss units (the Mothership stops launching while disabled). It disabled 145 tracks and helpers made 17 kills on them. Outside boss waves it adds little.
- Cluster (Champion only), EMP, dart and net are each bought by one or two levels, so their rows are small samples. For what a weapon is worth when it is always loaded, use `node tools/zombies-arsenal-bench.mjs --configs force:X`.

**Only matter against specific enemies.** Net: slow Shaheds. Laser, microwave, barrage, cluster, Airstrike: swarm drones. Pod: cruise missiles and jets. Dart: cruise, ballistic, boss. EMP: boss units. SAM: Shaheds, jets, decoys. Iron Dome: cruise missiles. Golden Dome: ballistic, hypersonic and ICBM bodies.
