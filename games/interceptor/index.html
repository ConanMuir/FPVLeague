INTERCEPTOR

Fly a Ukrainian-style tail-sitter dart interceptor (about 360 km/h level at 85° of pitch, hover around 32% throttle) against Shahed-136 and jet Geran-3 attack drones before they reach the substation, the fuel depot and the apartment blocks. You sit on the launch mat at a forward position on the river line, take off vertically, flip the camera to 90° and run them down. Same acro flight model, rates and radio setup as Drone League, at real scale, with weather and wind.

Shaheds come in from the north at 100–900 m, about 185 km/h, and dive on their target from about a kilometre out. Jet Geran-3s run at 300–370 km/h but throttle back to piston speed in the middle of the route, which is your window. The radar scope shows every track; the amber one is the most urgent. Trees and houses are solid. With FPV angle mix on, roll stick still rolls the picture when the camera is at 90°.
