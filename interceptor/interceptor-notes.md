# Drone League Interceptor: how it is built

- `drone-interceptor.html` is the game (artifact https://claude.ai/artifact/C8EZ2nNsYYywsCfgDw7sfX). `standalone/index.html` is the same file wrapped in a full HTML document; rebuild it after each change.
- Separate from `drone-league.html`. The flight model (`Drone.step/pivot/ground`, `rate()`, `thrCurve()`, `attitude()`, `humanControl()`, calibration, rates tab, CLI import/export) is copied from Drone League. When the League flight model changes, port the changed lines here.
- Settings key `dl-interceptor`. Defaults match Riley's League defaults (FOV 110, full-range throttle, InterLink calibration, rate profiles). New v3 settings: `wx` weather (default random), `rr` radar range index (2/5/10 km).
- v3 (Strila-class, real metres): thrust 50, curve 1.4, VP 260, drag .015/.0018, cap 112 m/s, stick lag 14 ms, spool 45 ms → ~360 km/h level at ~57° pitch, 0–200 km/h ≈ 1.6 s, hover ≈ 31%. Drag uses airspeed (v − WIND). Battery 12S 16000 mAh, current 6+220·ms^1.5 A; flat = lost. Catapult launch from the pad rail (CAT). `S.physV` 3 resets the Interceptor/Warhead mod groups. Preset "League 6S 5-inch" keeps League's real-scale numbers.
- v4 (tail-sitter dart): body +Y = fuselage/nose (thrust axis), camera on a tilt servo in the nose at body (0,.37,-.03). Drag: axial `DL+DQ·s`, sideways `DL+DQ·s·FS` (fins, mod `fs` 4) → 359 km/h level at 85° pitch, 0–200 km/h ≈ 1.6 s, hover ≈ 32% (thrust 48, dl .02, dq .0027, maxv 115). Crash speed 12 m/s, ground grip .5. `S.physV` 4 resets Interceptor/Warhead. Hull `HBP` = 4 fin tips (stands on them), 4 prop tips, nose. Standing upright below half-hover thrust the sticks are ignored (no tipping on the mat); pivot/turtle only once fallen over. `fwdH()` gives heading from body (0,1,-1) so it works hovering and in cruise.
- Camera: `camUp` runtime switch (L key or `S.tiltSw`) between `S.tilt` (default 90, `S.setV` 4 migrated Riley's 30 → 90) and level; spawns level. `S.fpvMix` mixes roll/yaw through the current camera angle (Betaflight fpv_mix_degrees). Thermal `therm` (H or `S.thermSw`): `wxLook(true)` blacks out sky/sun/cloud and sets emissive heat on `SHEN` (Shahed engine/jet pod), truck hood, `MS.met`, Shahed bodies; `sens` ShaderPass (after OutputPass, FPV only) does grain (`S.grain`), torn lines, rolling band, vignette and white-hot luminance. Switch mapping: -1 none, button index (toggle), or 'aN' axis (position).
- Forward positions `FOBS` (WEST/CENTRE/EAST, z = rivZ+330, in SOLG so ground is flat and tree-free): launch mat `f.mat`, pickup under a camo net, mast, crates. The player spawns standing on the mat of the FOB nearest `urgent()`, facing it, motors off; match start spawns the first track immediately so there is one. Keyboard throttle stays at idle until W is pressed (`kbArm`). Old catapult removed; PADP is now just the radar site.
- Trees and houses are solid: `sdObs()` over a 50 m grid `OG` (trunk cylinder + two crown lobes; walls + roof boxes), only checked within 26 m of the ground.
- World: AO ±3000 m (`AW`), terrain 9 km / 360 segments, ceiling 4000 m. `gh()` = exact mesh height. River ribbon is the only water. Sites in the south (z 1750–2180), pad z 1250. Terrain detail via `detail()` onBeforeCompile. Trees (≤17k) and houses instanced and solid, pylons visual only.
- Weather `WXS` presets via `setWeather()`: Preetham Sky + PMREM env, sprite cumulus, overcast deck plane (sky hidden, background = fog colour), rain line streaks around the camera, lightning + thunder in storm, `WIND` with gusts (affects drone, Shaheds, smoke).
- Threats: Shahed-136 real size (3.5 m, 2.5 m span) ~51 m/s; Geran-3 jet (3 m span, top turbojet) 92 m/s, slows to 60% on the middle leg. 80–900 m AGL, dog-leg waypoints, dive inside max(350, 1.1·AGL), dive speed up to 1.45×. Track ids `t.id`. Dead threats return early in `threatStep` (else they re-impact before `visuals()` removes them).
- Warhead: contact <2 m; proximity fuse at closest approach inside `fuseR(cv)=LETH·cl(1.3−cv/70,.15,1)` (LETH 4 m), so tail chase works and head-on needs a direct hit. Miss feed message when closing speed was too high.
- HUD: heading-up radar scope (`radar()`, sweep-updated blips, leaders, T## + altitude in hundreds of m, amber = `urgent()`), track box `#trk` with aspect/closing/fuse window, Betaflight-style horizon on the overlay, pursuit cue 40 m behind the selected target. OSD has BAT/mAh/timer, wind, weather.
- v5: midcourse guidance `guidance()` turns the selected track's last radar plot (`t.rb`, dead-reckoned) into a PIP `GD.pip`; MIDCOURSE until 1.5 km then TERMINAL; `S.guide` (G / `guideSw`), `nextTrack()` (N / `nextSw`), `selT()` = picked track or `urgent()`. Feed shader `sens` adds barrel distortion, chroma fringing, shadow noise and link breakup from `lq`. `linkQ()` = LQ from the home FOB antenna: falls off past 1.8 km, ×.35 when terrain blocks line of sight; OSD shows LQ%. Switch mapping `SWS` with Learn (`learnTick`): any new button, or an axis (not a stick axis) moving >.6 = 'aN'/'-aN' for that position. Assign switches (`asnStep`) walks `SWS` in order with `learn.seq`; each step takes its baseline 400 ms after the last capture so a settling switch isn't assigned twice; any key or Skip skips, Esc stops; Start doesn't close the menu while learning. Fixed the OSD clock (`this.t+=dt`).
- Testing: `dbg.html` exposes `window.__g`; Playwright + swiftshader, three routed to a local npm pack, fast-forward with `step(1/120)`.
