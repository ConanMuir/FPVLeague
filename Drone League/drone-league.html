Controller

RC radio or sim dongle: plug it in by USB and choose “USB Joystick (HID)” on the radio (EdgeTX / OpenTX / Ethos). Move a stick so the browser sees it, then press Calibrate controller and follow the prompts. Xbox controller: works straight away (left stick throttle, centre = hover); calibrate it to put throttle on a trigger or bumper, or yaw on the bumpers (LB/RB) or triggers. It keeps its own setup, separate from your radio. Gamepad shortcut buttons are ignored for radios so switches don't trigger them.
