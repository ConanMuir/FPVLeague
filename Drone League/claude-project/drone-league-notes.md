# Drone League: how the game is built

## Files
- `drone-league.html` is the game. On claude.ai it is published as an artifact page, without `<!doctype>`/`<html>`/`<head>` tags, because the artifact adds them.
- `standalone/index.html` is the same file wrapped in a full HTML document so it can run on its own. Rebuild it after every change to `drone-league.html`: add `<!doctype html><html lang="en"><head><meta charset="utf-8"><meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width,initial-scale=1,viewport-fit=cover"></head><body>` before it and `</body></html>` after it.
- `Play Drone League.bat` opens `standalone/index.html` in a Microsoft Edge app window.

## Code layout (sections marked with `// ---------- name ----------`)
1. **Constants and settings.** Arena size (W 220 × L 280 × H 64 m, Rocket League proportions relative to the ball; goals 46 × 18 × 14 m; corner radius RC 32, ramp radius RF 10). Spawns, orbs, floor markings (`pitchLines()`) and cameras are written relative to these. `DEF` holds the default settings, saved in the browser under the key `droneleague`.
2. **Rates.** `rate(axis, x)` implements Betaflight's rate formulas for BETAFLIGHT, ACTUAL, QUICK, KISS and RACEFLIGHT, returning rad/s; axis 0 is roll, 1 pitch, 2 yaw. `thrCurve()` applies throttle mid, expo and limit. There are three rate profiles in `S.rprof`, and `S.rpi` is the active one.
3. **Renderer.** Three.js with an UnrealBloom glow pass and ACES tone mapping.
4. **Arena.** `contour()` traces the rounded floor plan and `band()` builds the curved ramp and glass walls from the profile in `prof`. The goal mouths are cut out. The stadium crowd, light towers and stars come after that, in `neonG`.
4b. **Maps.** `OUT` holds the outdoor recipes (Meadow, Canyon, Alpine: sky, sun, turf, terrain, props); `LOOKS` holds per-map lighting, fog, shadow frustum and bloom. `buildOutdoor()` builds a map on first use into `mapG[id]`; `setMap()` swaps the floor, ramp, wall, goal-mouth and goal-floor materials and toggles `neonG` / the map groups / `snowP`. Maps are visual only and share the arena collision shape. The map choice is local, not synced online.
5. **Physics.** The arena collision shape is a signed distance function, `sdf()`. It is the rounded arena combined with the goal boxes, which extend into the field so the mouth stays open. `arena()` resolves sphere collisions with bounce and friction. `ballPhys()` moves the ball. Drone-ball touches use the drone's box (`BBX`, `ballBox()`), restitution `TB` (Touch bounce) at low speed rising to 0.6 at 36 m/s, a Rocket League-style extra push along drone→ball leaning toward the flight direction, and a little grip on soft touches for dribbling.
5b. **Ball styles.** `BSTY` builds Classic, Soccer (truncated-icosahedron shader), Plasma and Beach looks; `setBall()` swaps them. Visual only.
6. **Drones.** `Drone.step()` is the acro flight model. Sticks set target body rates and the drone eases toward them over `TAU`. LiftOff-style touches: motor spool (`SPOOL`), thrust falling off with axial inflow, body-axis drag (prop disc ~2x the frontal area), prop wash wobble when descending into it, and a soft speed cap. Ground contact is a rounded box (`HBP`: 16 spheres of radius `HBR` along a rounded-rectangle outline, 0.34 m corner radius, built in `applyTune()`; `ground()`) with shared impulses and lever arms and low sliding friction (`GF`, Ground grip); while touching with too little lift, `pivot()` levers the frame over its lowest edge at `TURT` (turtle mode without a switch). Boost: the top `BZ` (5%) of the throttle stick, or Shift/Space, is super boost (`BOOST` 140 m/s² on top with no spool or inflow loss, a `BKICK` 8 m/s kick when it lights, 1.5x speed cap, +14° FOV stretch via `bfov`) burning `DRAIN`/s; the rest of the stick maps to 0–100% motor. `ORBS` (8 big, 26 small) refill the meter.
7. **Attitude controller.** `attitude()` turns a target up/forward direction into body rates. Bots and Angle mode use it.
8. **Input.** Gamepad or radio through the browser's Gamepad API. `S.cal` stores the calibrated axis, centre and signed range for throttle, yaw, pitch and roll, and keyboard input is mixed in.
9. **Bots.** `predict()` simulates the ball 4 s ahead and `intercept()` finds the earliest point a bot can reach. `think()` handles attack, keep, mid and boost roles (attack lines up behind the ball, then commits to a strike with hysteresis, `ai.strike`), `assignRoles()` reassigns them every ~0.4 s, and `botControl()` is a cascaded controller (position error → velocity command capped at `DIFF[].vmax` → acceleration; pure pursuit at the contact point while striking) feeding `attitude()`. `DIFF` holds the seven skill levels (the seventh, Dribbler, is a mechanics demo: see *Dribbler bot* below). Champion and Grand Champion (`DIFF[].iq`) use the shot planner at the end of `bots/league.js`: `plan()` scans the 50 Hz prediction (`PRED`, velocities `PV`), aims the contact normal at (shot direction * shot speed - ball velocity), keeps the contact point and a 10 m run-up off the floor and walls, and picks the earliest point it can reach from behind (`reachT`); if a foe can touch first it takes the quickest touch instead; `strikeV` flies a timed run through the contact point (`ka`, `fl`); `rolesIQ` assigns roles by reach time. Grand Champion adds `aim` (`bestAim`: the goal spot defenders can least reach in time), `demo` (the third man demolishes the keeper while the ball is in the attacking quarter) and `pop` (bots earn pops like players, `d.pops`, and `popShot` spends one when a pop direction points at the goal). In Dogfight, `iq` bots lead with the exact bullet intercept plus the target's acceleration (`leadIQ`), sit on the target's six (`sixIQ`) and jink across the line of fire when someone has them in their guns (`threatOf`, `jinkIQ`). `node tools/bot-match.mjs [league|df|drill] A B minutes size` plays two levels against each other headless (half on each side, stock parts).
9. **Bots.** `predict()` simulates the ball 4 s ahead and `intercept()` finds the earliest point a bot can reach. `think()` handles attack, keep, mid and boost roles (attack lines up behind the ball, then commits to a strike with hysteresis, `ai.strike`), `assignRoles()` reassigns them every ~0.4 s, and `botControl()` is a cascaded controller (position error → velocity command capped at `DIFF[].vmax` → acceleration; pure pursuit at the contact point while striking) feeding `attitude()`. `DIFF` holds the four skill levels.
10. **Game state.** Kickoff, play, goal and end states, slots, demolitions and the boost orbs.
11. **Online play.** The host simulates the ball, score and bots. Each pilot flies their own drone locally and sends position, attitude and velocity about 30 times a second, plus a sequence-numbered ball-hit message the host acknowledges. On claude.ai this runs over the artifact `room` channel. Outside claude.ai, `p2pRoom()` provides the same calls over PeerJS/WebRTC, with the host relaying to everyone. Slot keys are `h` for the host, `bTI` for bots and a peer label for each human.
12. **Mod panel.** The Rates tab works like Betaflight Configurator, including CLI import and export. The Physics tab has 25 sliders defined in `MODS`, with presets and export/import. In online games the host's physics settings apply to everyone.
12b. **Dogfight mode.** `S.game` ('league' | 'df') picks the mode from the two buttons at the top of the menu; `setGame()` switches `GM`/`OBSON`, swaps to the Highlands map (`setMap('df')`, `buildDF()` builds its meshes on first use into `dfG`), hides `goalG`, the arena and the ball, and moves the orbs to `o.dp`. Highlands: 1100 x 1400 m (`DW`, `DLEN`), ceiling `DCEIL` 260, terrain `dfH(x,z)` (fbm hills, river canyon along `cxR(z)` at y -40, mountain walls at the edges, flat team bases at z = ±`DBASE`), all point-symmetric. `OBS` holds mesas and spire fields (cyl), hangars open at both ends (hang), towers and canyon bridges (box), rock arches (ring), boulders and ~480 trees, seeded and mirrored; `OGRID` is a 40 m spatial hash, so `sdObs` is exact only within 10 m. `sdDF` = max(ceiling, walls, terrain, -obstacles) replaces `sdf` in Dogfight. `los()` sphere-traces line of sight: turrets (6 per team, 200 m) and bots only shoot what they can see. Guns fire along the FPV camera axis (`gunDir`; bots use 30°): 12 rounds/s, 320 m/s plus the shooter's velocity, 8 damage, hit radius 1.6 m, 100 HP, 3 s respawn at the base (`DSLOTS`). A kill or a turret is 1 point. `dfBot()` scores targets by distance, facing and visibility, cruises at 60 m/s when far, holds ~40 m, avoids terrain with two look-ahead probes, and in range tips the quad so the uptilted camera points at the lead. Fire input: F, left mouse, `S.fireBtn`, and `S.afire` auto-fire. Online: the host owns HP, deaths (`g.lf`), turrets (`g.tg`) and the kill feed (`g.kf`); joiners report their own hits as `P.hs`, and send `P.f`/`P.ti`. `g.m` carries the mode.
12d. **Dogfight visuals and bot feel.** Tracers are instanced additive streaks (`BSTR`, width ~0.5% of camera distance) plus thin lines. Enemy drones draw 1.6x larger in Dogfight and every drone's bullet hit radius is 2 m. `RIML` holds the arena rim lines, hidden in Highlands. `dfBot` flies nose-first (heading follows the thrust tilt, yaws to the target only near level), smooths sticks and throttle like a pilot (`ai.ps`, ~60–110 ms), and aims with a wandering error that grows with range (`ai.nz`).
12e. **Guns, fire input, snow.** Twin muzzles under the frame (body offset ±0.4, -0.55, -0.3), alternating, converging on the camera axis 150 m out; tracer tails are clamped to distance flown (`b.dist`) and your own rounds fade in over the first 30 m. `S.fireInv` inverts the Fire button/switch. Alpine snow (`snowP`) is in world space and wraps in a 160 m box around the camera.
12c. **Super boost toggle.** `S.sboost` (default off). `SB()` is the live value (the host's `g.sb` for joiners). Off: the whole throttle stick is 0–100% motor, Shift/Space do nothing, bots never boost, the boost bar and orbs are hidden.

## Gotchas
- The page relies on the CSS rule `[hidden]{display:none!important}`. Keep it.
- Anything received from other players is untrusted. Validate the numbers and set names with `textContent`.
- After editing, check that the script parses, for example by extracting the module script and running `node --check`.
- Bot slot keys are `b` + team + index; `isBot` must be `/^b[01]\d$/` (an earlier copy had lost the backslash, so bots mirrored the player's sticks).

## Physics: Rapier, curved arena, hitboxes (v16, track B)
- **Loop.** `main.js` steps the sim at a fixed 120 Hz (`tick()`; Game speed / slow-mo / pause change how much sim time a frame covers) and draws drones and the ball interpolated between the last two ticks (`lerpIn`/`lerpOut` swap `p`/`q` around `visuals()`, `updateCam()`, `hud()`; remote drones are left to `visuals()`). Rapier (`@dimforge/rapier3d-compat` 0.14.0, WASM inlined as base64, so the single file still needs no network) initialises before boot (`initPhysics().then(...)`).
- **State stays in JS.** `d.p q v w` and `ball.p v w q` are the truth. `physStep(dt, simBall)` (once per `step()`, after controls and the flight model) writes them into the bodies, steps the Rapier world, reads them back. So spawns, kickoffs, demolitions, online corrections and the Calibration Lab's rigs keep working by setting `d.p` etc. Bodies: dynamic when stepped this tick (`d.ph`, set by `Drone.step`), kinematic when remote or frozen at kickoff, disabled when dead. World gravity is 0 (drones get `G`, the ball `BG`, from our code).
- **Drones.** `Drone.step()` is still the whole flight model (thrust, spool, inflow, drag, boost, wash, rates, ground-rate damping, turtle `pivot()` and the v16 pendulum); it sets `v` and body rates `w` and leaves `q` at the tick's start (thrust and drag use the attitude at the end, as v15). Rapier integrates `p += v*dt`, `q` by `w*dt` (free flight matches the v15 integration to 0.03 mm over a 1 s punch) and resolves contact with the world: CCD on, restitution `1.15*(1+DB)-1` (the old impulse factor), no solver friction. Ground friction is ours (`groundContacts`): Coulomb `GF` on Rapier's normal impulse, capped at `G*.05` per tick so thrusting into the ground adds no grip (v17), spin from it x.2 (v15); it also sets the ground timer `gt` and the impact speed `d.hit` (bump sound in `step.js`). `pivot()` uses the descriptor's contact points (`d.pts`, stock: v15's 16 hull points) against the SDF and then lets Rapier move the quad. Safety net: a drone whose centre ends up inside the map (SDF; Highlands: terrain/walls/ceiling only) is pushed back (`PHYS.stats.escapes`).
- **Drone colliders (interface for parts, `physics/world.js`).** `buildDroneBody(drone, desc)` (re)builds `drone.rb` (the Rapier body) from `desc = {mass, inertia:[ix,iy,iz]|null, pivot?, colliders:[{shape:'box'|'capsule'|'sphere'|'cylinder'|'hull', size, pos:[x,y,z], rot:[x,y,z,w], tag:'chassis'|'arm'|'prop'|'camera'|'other', points?, round?, use?}]}`; No `desc` = the drone's parts loadout, `loadoutDesc(d.lo)`: the parts' colliders from `bodyOf(lo)` (chassis, battery, camera, arms, prop discs; tags reported) take ball touches, drone bumps and bullets, and a rounded box fitted to the loadout's ground hull (`lo.hbp/hbr`) is the 'world' contact, so ground feel stays v15's and scales with the parts; mass from the parts, inertia `mass*di/.45` split over the axes like the parts' real inertia. `Drone.setLoadout()` rebuilds the body (also on tune changes). `defaultDroneDesc()` is the v15 stock quad (checks use it for parity). Sizes: box `[hx,hy,hz]` half extents, sphere `[r]`, capsule/cylinder `[halfHeight, r]` along local Y, hull `points` (flat array or `[[x,y,z],…]`); `round` = edge radius. `use` (default all): `'world'` solid contact with arena/terrain (these carry the mass when `inertia` is null and give pivot points), `'ball'` ball touches, `'drone'` bumps/demolitions, `'bullet'` hits. `inertia` null = from the 'world' colliders at uniform density. The stock quad: a rounded box 1.1 x 0.26 x 1.2 m (edge radius `HBR`; 'world', tag chassis), v15's ball box `BBX` ('ball','bullet', chassis) and the `DRAD` sphere ('drone', other); mass 1, inertia `DI/.45` (v15's contact spin factor). Use rounded boxes/spheres/capsules for 'world' colliders: hull facets can tilt the contact normal of a flat face sliding on the floor (a small bounce). Rapier 0.14's round convex hulls/cylinders sink by most of their radius into a trimesh with internal-edge fixing, so rounded hulls/cylinders are built as plain hulls sampled from the rounded shape. Events: `onHit(fn)` gets `{type:'ball'|'drone'|'bullet', d, tag, other, otherTag, speed}`.
- **Touches.** `ballTouch(d, n)` and `droneTouch(a, b, n)` (with a 0.4 m contact skin, `BUMP`: thin frames need some, v15's 1.2 m spheres were generous) are Rapier shape-vs-shape queries on the drone's 'ball' / 'drone' colliders returning the normal, depth and tag; `step.js` runs the v15 responses on them (TB restitution curve, RL-style push, dribble grip; drone bumps x1.5 and demolitions above `DEMO`) with the parts' mass, kick and grip. With `defaultDroneDesc()` they give exactly v15's `ballBox()` and sphere results.
- **Arena (`physics/arena-shape.js`).** One parametric profile builds the visual pieces (`world/arena.js`), the collision trimesh and the analytic SDF `arenaSD()`. W/L/H, goal size, `RF` 10 and `RC` 32 are unchanged (spawns, orbs, pitch lines line up). New: the goal mouth rim is rounded with `RM` 2.5 m (posts, crossbar, and the cut in the quarter-pipe beside the mouth: an exact rolling-ball fillet, since the arena surface and the goal walls always meet at 90°) and the goal pocket has rounded back and top edges (`RG` 3 m). The surface is a grid of U-shaped rings around each mouth (arena side, fillet angle, goal side, back fillet, back wall) stitched to the band (columns x profile rows: 32 per quarter-pipe, 48 per plan corner); seams share vertices bit for bit, so the welded mesh is closed and consistently wound (`tools/physics-check.mjs` checks it) and lies on the SDF to < 0.01 mm at vertices, < 2 cm at chords. Normals are analytic. Collision: the trimesh (`TriMeshFlags.FIX_INTERNAL_EDGES`) without the flat floor and ceiling, which are boxes (a 4 m strip along their edges stays in the mesh so the seams are connected): earcut's 200 m sliver triangles made every query near the floor 10x slower. Posts get a glow tube along the middle of the rim (`AR.rim`); the rim and the ramp's cut faces use the ramp material, the pocket the net material.
- **Ball (`physics/ball.js`).** A Rapier dynamic body whose contact with the map is analytic against the SDF (exact normals, no facet bumps; the Rapier trimesh contact was measured and hops on facets, `PHYS.ballMesh` switches to it for comparison). `ballPre`: BG, BD, MAXB as v15. Impacts (a new contact > 1 m/s): restitution BB, Coulomb friction .25 at the contact point with spin. Staying on the same surface (normal turned < 25° since last tick): it rolls, its velocity turns with the surface normal (a curve changes direction, not speed), spin follows the roll, and a rolling resistance `.25*BG` acts on flat ground (v15's 4.9 m/s² roll-out), fading out by ~37° of slope. So the ball rolls up the quarter-pipe and wall and back with no loss beyond drag and that roll-out (v15 lost 80-95% to sliding friction). The ball now spins visibly (`ball.q`). Bots' `predict()` runs the same `ballPhys` at 50 Hz.
- **Highlands.** Terrain is a Rapier heightfield on the terrain mesh's own grid (`dfGrid()`, 280 x 350 cells over 2 x 2.3 km; three's PlaneGeometry splits cells along the same diagonal, so collision = the triangles you see). Obstacles get colliders from their meshes (`DFOBS`): mesas/spires and rocks as convex hulls of the mesh vertices, towers/bridges/hangar slabs as boxes, trees as a cylinder trunk + cone crown, arches as 40 capsules, the base pads as cylinders. `sdDF` uses the mesh heights (`dfHt`) once the map is built.
- **Bullets / line of sight.** Each tick a bullet casts a ray against the map and a 1.4 m sphere against the drones' 'bullet' colliders along its path (stock quad: hit within ~2.0-2.3 m of the centre, like v15's 2 m sphere; the tag is reported); turrets are still 2.6 m spheres; the nearest hit wins. `los()` is a Rapier ray (first and last 2 m ignored, as v15).
- **Checks.** `node tools/physics-check.mjs` (geometry in node; the rest in headless Edge against the Vite dev server, importing the live modules): mesh closure/orientation/SDF fit, ball up the quarter-pipe (analytic vs trimesh vs v15), drops on rims and corners, free flight vs v15 integration, ground rest/slide/turtle/self-righting vs a port of v17's `ground()`, bullets vs thin obstacles, heightfield vs mesh, predictor and touches vs v15, cost per tick. `--shots` writes goal-mouth screenshots to `shots/`.

- **Attachments (4th Garage slot, `parts/attach.js`):** None (default, neutral), Ram Bumper, Ball Cage, Prop Ducts, Armor Plates. Pure data folded into `computeLoadout`: mass placed where it sits (so inertia/agility change), drag, thrust (ducts), spool, ball grip, touch kick, extra colliders (tag `other`; ducts widen the prop colliders), and `flight.armor` (x damage in Dogfight, applied in `hitDrone` on the host). Loadouts are 4 ids; 3-id saves and peers get `none`. `parts-balance` checks each attachment against None on a Stock base. New Garage stat: Toughness.
- **Freeplay** (`modes/freeplay.js`, `free:true` in the registry): only the player, no clock (HUD shows FREEPLAY), goals still reset the ball. Reset (R / Xbox Y) works only in Freeplay (`canReset` in input.js). The minimap (radar) was removed from every mode.
- **Calibration Lab, live tuning (v2):** the dodge drill is gone (6 drills). Changes are judged after one more rep (was two), so the tune keeps moving. Small corrections are scored every 2.5 s (was 5 s). New live **hold** check (`holdMetrics` / `onHold`) every ~1.5 s in the hover and altitude drills: bouncing through the band adds throttle expo; sinking or climbing with the stick held still moves throttle mid to the motor actually needed; stick pinned at an end and still outside the band removes expo.
- **Dogfight respawns:** `dfSpawnPoint(others)` in world/highlands.js picks from spawn points spread over the valley (every 120 x 130 m, 30 m over the ground, beyond sdObs range from obstacles), the one farthest from everyone alive (random among the best three), facing the valley centre. `Drone.spawn(true)` uses it after a kill; kickoff still uses the team bases (DSLOTS).
- **Pop** (`Drone.tryPop` / `popZone` in flight/drone.js, earning in modes/step.js, ring in render/popfx.js, check `node tools/pop-check.mjs`): the ball touching the top of the quad (contact normal within ~40 deg of up) inside the square of its four prop colliders (inset 10%) earns a pop for POP_T 5 s (a ring when newly earned, see *Touch and pop rings*; the dial shows POP). - **Ground handling (v5):** Ground grip default 0.1 -> 0.7 (physV 5 migrates untouched saves). Friction acts at every contact point with its share of the normal impulse (so rolling over an edge is not braked), removes at most 70% of the slip per step (no ringing), spins the frame little when nearly still and fully when sliding, grows with slide speed for a bare frame, and a bare frame tilted >~14 deg while sliding >12 m/s gets a leading-edge catch that tumbles it. Sliding faster than 4 m/s, the motors only fight a tumble as hard as their spin allows. The v20 pendulum keeps rolling under motor power after the pivot loses the ground. **Wheels** attachment (`wheels:true`): roll along the forward axis (mu .03), grip sideways (max(GF, .9)), barely spin the frame, and keep the old planted ground rate; Ground drive stat.
- **Ball touches (v2):** touch bounce is one smooth curve from 2 x Touch bounce (soft) to 0.6 with hit speed (no dead zone below 6 m/s); the RL extra impulse ramps in smoothly with relative speed. The velocity "glue" is replaced by contact friction (mu .15 + .25 x chassis grip, sphere tangential mass 3.5/m), so glancing hits spin the ball, plus rolling resistance on resting carries so a balanced dribble rides along and a hard push dislodges it. Pops keep the contact model they were tuned with (old restitution, extra impulse and frame grip). `pop-check` also asserts pop directions.
