Controller

RC radio or sim dongle: plug it in by USB and choose “USB Joystick (HID)” on the radio (EdgeTX / OpenTX / Ethos). Move a stick so the browser sees it, then press Choose your controls and move or press whatever you want for each function. Xbox controller: works straight away (left stick throttle, centre = hover); choose your controls to put any function on any stick, trigger, bumper, d-pad or button. It keeps its own setup, separate from your radio. Gamepad shortcut buttons are ignored for radios so switches don't trigger them.
